Factions:
Federacion de la Nueva Tierra (FNT) soldiers
Exclusionary Megaregion (EMR) Terrorists/Crimelords

In the year 2202, most of the Earth lives in a Solar Punk haven where poverty is non-existent 
and crime is a near-statistic impossibility.

However, 400 million live in a wasteland, on the Earth's north-western hemisphere due to pol-
itical and socio-econmic decay in that region.

To keep crime at an all time low in the Federacion, there is an annual blood sport where the
elite soldiers of the Federacion, some of whom are latent criminals themselves, indulge in their lust for blood and violence as they take on the terrorists and warlords that inhabit the Exclusionary Megaregion.
